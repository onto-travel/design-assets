Good to know

Check-in from 2:00 PM · check-out by 12:00 noon.

Up to two children under 5 stay free using existing beds. A rollaway/extra bed is about ₹4,000 per night.

Cashless property — Visa, Mastercard and Amex accepted; no cash.

Rates shown are non-refundable. Add free cancellation per room at checkout for flexibility.

Buffet breakfast, if not included, runs roughly ₹1,500 for adults and ₹750 for children.

Cats and dogs under 30 lb are welcome (up to two per room) in garden-view villa categories only, for a non-refundable fee of about ₹2,000 plus taxes per pet, per night.

Pets are not permitted in restaurants, the lobby, the pool or the gym.

About 45 minutes by car from Dabolim Airport, or roughly 1.5 hours from Manohar International Airport at Mopa.

7.5 miles from Madgaon (Margao) city. Free parking is available on site.
